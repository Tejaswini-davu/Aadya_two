Who We Are

Your Trusted Bridge to
Authentic South Asian
Grocery Excellence

Aadya Foods LLC is a full-service importer, manufacturer, wholesaler, and distributor of South Asian food and grocery products, proudly headquartered in Dulles, Virginia.

We serve the entire East Coast of the USA, striving to meet the needs of customers with a wide range of the finest products sourced from around the world — from spices and pulses, to grains, oils, and much more. Whether you are a wholesaler, retailer, restaurant, food service provider, or manufacturer, Aadya Foods has the supplies to fulfill your needs.
